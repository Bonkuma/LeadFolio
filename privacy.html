LeadFolio

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

LeadFolio keeps only what you put into it, uses it only to run the app for you, and never sells it, shares it for advertising or tracks you.

LeadFolio is an app for people managers: 1:1 notes, follow-ups, meetings, strategy and reflection. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and the choices you have. Questions: contact@leadfolio.co.

What LeadFolio stores

WhatWhy
Your email address, and a passkey's public key if you add oneTo sign you in with a one-time code or a passkey, and identify your account
What you enter: people, 1:1 agendas and notes, Pulse Points, follow-ups, meetings, shout-outs, strategy, reflections and quotesTo show it back to you on your devices
Handwritten notes and imported PDFs, plus the text read from themTo show them back to you and make them searchable
Your settings (for example, first day of the week)To keep the app set up the way you like

LeadFolio does not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers or device analytics. It has no ads and no third-party tracking or analytics tools.

The assistant runs on your device

Summaries, suggested follow-ups, agenda ideas, recaps and handwriting reading all run on your iPhone or iPad, using Apple's on-device model and handwriting recognition. Your notes are not sent to LeadFolio or to any AI company for these features. You can turn the assistant off in Settings.

LeadFolio Pro, a planned paid upgrade, may later offer features powered by Claude from Anthropic. Those would be off unless you turn them on. Before anything is sent, this policy will be updated to say what is sent and how it is handled.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, the cloud database provider that runs LeadFolio's servers, and is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only read its own data; this is enforced in the database, not just in the app. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's keychain. If you add a passkey, its private key is created and kept by your device and iCloud Keychain; LeadFolio stores only the public part.

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use it to train AI models.
  • Service providers (Supabase for storage and sign-in, and the email service that delivers your sign-in code) handle it only to run LeadFolio.
  • We may disclose data only if the law requires it, and we'll tell you when we're allowed to.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › Export my data gives you everything as a readable document or a data file (JSON).
  • Delete: Settings › Delete account permanently deletes your account, everything you entered and your imported PDFs, right away. It can't be undone.
  • Edit or remove anything in the app at any time; deleted items are removed from our database.
  • Turn off the assistant in Settings.

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. Backups kept by our database provider may hold deleted data for a short time before they expire. You can also email contact@leadfolio.co to ask for a copy or deletion.

People you write about

Your notes may mention your team members. That information is stored in your account only, is visible only to you, and is deleted when you delete it or your account. LeadFolio doesn't contact them or share it with them. Please keep notes professional and follow your employer's policies.

Children

LeadFolio is meant for working adults and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Email contact@leadfolio.co with any privacy question or request.
